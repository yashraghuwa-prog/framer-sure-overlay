Patented · 30-Hour Vedic Time System

Aarohanam Labs has revived the world’s oldest cosmology of time — a 30-hour day, anchored to the sun, divided into 30 muhurats — and rebuilt it as a living digital instrument. From the first tower clock at Ujjain to the device on your wrist.

The Idea & The Patent

A clock that turns

with the sun.

A clock that turns

with the sun.

For five thousand years, India measured time not by the swing of a

pendulum but by the rising of the sun. Sūryodaya to sūryodaya —

one full day, divided into thirty equal muhurats. We hold the patent

on the digital reconstruction of that system.

Vedic · Muhurats

Recognition & Journey

From a foundation stone

to a national symbol.

From a foundation stone to a national symbol.

Three years. Three sacred installations. One ancient idea, returning.

Each tower is more than a timekeeper. It is a public reassertion that India’s astronomical heritage is precise, alive, and worth standing 85 feet tall for.
